# FAQ — CCAR-P (Professional)

**Is this an official Anthropic resource?**
No. It's an independent, community-maintained study aid written by an architect who holds both the Foundations and the Professional certification. It's built from Anthropic's public exam guide and documentation and isn't reviewed or endorsed by Anthropic. Always defer to the [official exam guide](resources.md#official-anthropic-exam-resources) on policy.

**Does it contain real exam questions?**
No — never. Every question is original and written against the public blueprint. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#the-one-hard-rule). The exam's NDA means real items must never be shared, and using leaked items to study violates it too.

**Which exam version does this cover?**
**v1.1, effective December 7, 2026** (70 items, 7 domains, 39 skills). If you sit the exam before that date you get v1.0 (63 items). The [What changed](about-the-exam.md#what-changed-from-v10-to-v11) table shows the differences, and most of this guide still applies to v1.0. Skip the regulation-name trivia v1.0 tested (GDPR/HIPAA/FedRAMP) only if you're on v1.1.

**Do I need Foundations (CCAR-F) first?**
It's not required, but the v1.1 guide says the Foundations scope is **assumed knowledge**. If the agentic loop, hooks vs. prompts, tool descriptions, or structured output feel shaky, do this repo's [Foundations guide](../../study-guide/) first.

**Who can take CCAR-P?**
Claude Partner Network members registering with a partner company email domain, aged 18+, in supported countries. Check the [certification FAQ](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/faq-certifications).

**Is there an official practice exam?**
Not any more — it was retired in the move to Pearson. The guide's three sample questions are the only official items. This repo's [three mock exams](../mock-exam/) (210 original questions) fill that gap.

**How hard are the mock exams compared to the real one?**
Exam 1 is a medium on-ramp. Exams 2 and 3 are calibrated to the v1.1 style: long evidence-based stems, close distractors, some arithmetic. A strong mock score is a good sign, not a guarantee.

**What's the single most useful habit?**
Name the binding constraint before you read the options, then eliminate anything that instructs instead of enforces. See the [cheat sheet](cheat-sheet.md).

**Product details change fast — how current is this?**
Facts were checked against Anthropic's documentation in November 2026. Features, model names, and prices move; the exam tests *architectural judgment*, so this guide states prices inside examples rather than relying on a price list. If you spot drift, please open an issue or PR.

**Can I contribute?**
Yes — corrections, clearer explanations, new original questions. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md).
