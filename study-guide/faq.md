# FAQ

**Is this an official Anthropic resource?**
No. This is an independent, community-maintained study aid. It's built from Anthropic's publicly published Exam Guide, but it is not reviewed, endorsed, or affiliated with Anthropic. Always defer to the [official Exam Guide](resources.md#official-anthropic-resources) for anything policy-related (fees, scheduling, retake rules).

**Does this guide contain real exam questions?**
No, and it never will — see [CONTRIBUTING.md's hard rule](../CONTRIBUTING.md#the-one-hard-rule). Every question in this repo is original material written to test understanding of the public blueprint. Accepting the exam's NDA means you agree not to disclose real exam content, and using leaked content to study would violate that agreement even before you sit the exam.

**Does the mock exam guarantee I'll pass?**
No. It's built to mirror the real exam's format (60 items, 4-of-6 scenario structure, similar difficulty) and to stress the same judgment patterns the blueprint describes, but it cannot replicate the actual item bank. Treat a strong mock score as a good sign, not a guarantee.

**I don't have a strong ML/engineering background. Can I still pass?**
Yes — the exam tests architecture judgment (when to use a hook vs. a prompt, when to escalate, when plan mode is worth it), not machine learning theory or deep coding ability. See [About the Exam → Out of scope](about-the-exam.md#out-of-scope-dont-waste-study-time-here) for what you explicitly don't need to know.

**How current is this guide?**
Check [CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md) for the last update date and which Exam Guide version it was cross-checked against. Anthropic updates the exam blueprint periodically; if you notice this guide has drifted from the current official guide, please open an issue or PR.

**Can I contribute even if I haven't passed the exam yet?**
Yes. Corrections, clarity improvements, and new original practice questions are welcome from anyone — you don't need to have already passed. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

**Where do I actually register for the exam?**
Through the Anthropic Partner Academy and Pearson VUE — see [About the Exam → Registration and retakes](about-the-exam.md#registration-and-retakes).

**What if I fail?**
Retake waiting periods increase with each attempt: 14 days after the first, 30 after the second, 90 after the third, up to 4 attempts within a rolling 12 months. Use your score report's per-domain breakdown to target your re-study — it won't determine your pass/fail result, but it tells you exactly where to focus.
